About Me

Hi! I'm Yixuan Wu from Nanchang, Jiangxi, China. I'm currently pursuing my Master of Engineering in Financial Engineering at Cornell University. I received my Bachelor of Mathematics in Mathematical Finance & Statistics from the University of Waterloo, with a minor in Pure Mathematics.

My academic interests lie in mathematical analysis, statistical modeling, and quantitative finance. I'm passionate about applying mathematical modeling and machine learning to solve real-world financial problems in trading and risk management.

I have led and contributed to several projects, including designing a high-frequency limit order execution algorithm using ML signals, optimizing portfolios under liquidity-adjusted VaR constraints using regime-aware VIX indicators, and building time series and ARIMA-based models to forecast exchange rates. My work consistently balances theoretical rigor with practical value.

Professionally, I’ve interned across quantitative research, risk management, and big data roles. I have experience in factor mining, machine learning model developement, and risk management process automation.

Through these experiences, I’ve developed strong technical and analytical skills, as well as a deep understanding of how quantitative methods are applied in real-world finance.
